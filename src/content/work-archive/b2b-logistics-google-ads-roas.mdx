## The Challenge: Rising CPAs and Fragmented Conversion Attribution

FleetLogix was spending upwards of $60,000/month on Google Ads. However, campaign profitability had deteriorated:
- Cost Per Acquisition (CPA) had surged to $184 due to uncontrolled broad-match expansion.
- Client-side tracking scripts were losing up to 35% of conversion signals due to browser ad blockers and Safari ITP restrictions.
- Generic landing pages failed to answer specific procurement criteria, resulting in a sluggish 2.1% visitor-to-lead conversion rate.

## The Strategy: Intent Sculpting & Server-Side Measurement

We engineered a full Google Ads acquisition turnaround:

### 1. Negative Query Sculpting & STAG Migration
- Audited 12 months of search query reports and added 1,800+ negative keywords across logistics, freight brokerage, and fleet telematics.
- Restructured campaigns into **Single-Themed Ad Groups (STAGs)** with dedicated ad copy matching exact buyer intent (e.g. *Cold-Chain Temperature Monitoring Software* vs *Fleet Dispatch Routing API*).

### 2. Server-Side Google Tag Manager (sGTM) & Enhanced Conversions
- Deployed a custom server-side GTM container on Cloudflare, bypassing browser ad blockers.
- Configured Google Ads Enhanced Conversions API with hashed user telemetry, providing Smart Bidding algorithms with 100% accurate conversion value data.

### 3. High-Converting Static Landing Pages
- Built custom Astro landing pages with sub-second load speeds, interactive pricing calculators, and immediate value proposition answer blocks.

## The Results

- **ROAS Scaled to 5.2x**: Return on Ad Spend climbed from 1.8x to a verified 5.2x average across all active campaigns.
- **-42% CPA Reduction**: Cost per qualified procurement demo dropped from $184 to $98.
- **+185% Pipeline Increase**: Delivered 340+ validated enterprise logistics leads in the first quarter post-optimization.
